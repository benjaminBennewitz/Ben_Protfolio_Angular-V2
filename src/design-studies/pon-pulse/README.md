# PON! PULSE — ポン！パルス

[← Study overview](../../../README.md#standalone-design-studies) · [Open study](https://b2folio.de/design-studies/pon-pulse/)

PON! PULSE is a fictional graffiti artist spotlight in the style of a street-art shop editorial. It follows the writer Pon Pulse, alias PP, from a halftone portrait and a short biography to the concrete piece **Sugar Static**, an ultra-wide **Train Bomb** photograph and the kit she reaches for.

The visual system pairs a black and white grid and condensed title lettering with hot pink, cyan, Japanese typography and a bubblegum heart sticker. A white PポP handstyle marks the portrait. Two narrow Latin Ps frame the katakana ポ from Pon Pulse. Sugar Static uses the bubble-letter monogram on concrete. The train mural, **No Gods, No Bedtime**, combines painted lettering and a grinning manga rabbit. Its carriage is an imagined scene generated without a source image. The [JR West note on the orange JNR 103 series](https://www.westjr.co.jp/press/article/2017/10/page_11351.html) documents the type that informed the brief.

The equipment spread lists Montana GOLD cans, a respirator, fat and skinny caps and a blackbook. The flat lay is illustrative and the product mention is editorial. Pon Pulse, her biography and the works are fictional. The portrait, tag, works and flat lay are AI-generated imagery. The archive preview is a screenshot of the running page.

The study uses standalone HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Angular copies it through the existing static asset pipeline. Images open in a native dialog with keyboard dismissal or as direct WebP files via their links. German and English copy is available, while both languages preserve the shared Japanese and English display typography. The independent preferences inherit the portfolio choices until changed locally. The title assembles letter by letter, with a rolling exclamation point. Sugar Static starts as a flat concrete-colored surface. On entry, three timed fat-cap passes reveal the artwork over the empty wall. The finished picture is the static fallback, and a replay button restarts the sequence. The train slides in from the left and a text-only horn bubble appears above it. The two image entrances start once the scene reaches roughly the middle of the viewport. A short gum-pop plays on clicks or keyboard activation. Holding the primary mouse button grows a circular bubble to half the shorter viewport edge, decelerating as it approaches full size, then bursts it and briefly shakes the page. Releasing early removes the growing bubble. These animations respect the reduced-motion preference. Both language versions link to the portfolio’s legal notice and privacy policy in the footer.

Barlow Condensed and JetBrains Mono are reused from the portfolio. Pulse JP is a local subset of Noto Sans CJK JP Bold and retains its [SIL Open Font License](assets/OFL-Pulse-JP.txt).

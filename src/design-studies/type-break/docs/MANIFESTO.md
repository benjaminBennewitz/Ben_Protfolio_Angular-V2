# TYPE//BREAK

## NOTHING NEW WITHOUT BREAKING.

**An interactive manifesto on destruction, transformation, and creation.**

Nothing stays the same.
Not ideas. Not systems. Not the rules we inherit.

We build structures to make sense of the world.
We follow patterns because they feel familiar.
We call that stability.

But stability is not the same as progress.

**PRESSURE REVEALS THE CRACKS.**

A letter bends. A line slips. The grid starts to shift.
What once appeared permanent turns out to be movable.

**BREAKING IS NOT THE END.**

To break is to question what we took for granted.
To pull things apart is to see what they're made of.
To make room is to make possibility.

**DON'T RESTORE THE OLD FORM.**

Rearrange the fragments.
Change the message.
Build something that could not exist before.

**NOTHING NEW WITHOUT BREAKING.**

TYPE//BREAK — A design study in form, resistance, and renewal.

---

### Editorial note

This manifesto uses breaking as a metaphor for creative transformation, not as an endorsement of harm or destruction. The experience challenges visual conventions through interactive typography.

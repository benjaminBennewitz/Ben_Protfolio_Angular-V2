Datei: assets/sounds/town-square.mp3
Aktuell: enthaltene synthetisierte Stadt-Platzhaltermusik.

PROMPT:
Instrumental daytime village music for a cozy goblin guild town in a monochrome handheld fantasy RPG. Grassy lanes, old timber houses, a small stream and wooden bridge, cats, ducks, busy workshops and a welcoming tavern. Original Game Boy inspired chiptune with playful but gentle square-wave melody, rounded triangle bass and light noise-channel percussion. Warm folk-like phrasing, a little crooked and mischievous, never childish or overly cheerful. Medium relaxed 92 BPM, major and modal harmony, memorable understated melody, spacious enough for dialogue and exploration. Seamless loop structure with no intro, no ending, no fade-out. No vocals, no choir, no speech, no modern pop drums, no orchestral climax. Original melody.

ATMOSPHÄRE, SEPARAT:
Peaceful small medieval fantasy town on a mild spring day. A nearby flowing stream, soft leaves in a breeze, occasional small birds, a distant duck and a few quiet workshop taps. No intelligible speech, no crowd roar, no music, no footsteps, no traffic. Natural subtle layers, gentle steady ambience, loopable, no sudden loud sounds.
